# Onboarding and Probation Policy (Synthetic Demonstration Data)

**Company:** Northstar Maritime Systems (Fictional) | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> Notice: This is synthetic demonstration data and not a real employment policy.

## 1. Probation Duration and Milestones
- All newly hired full-time employees serve a **90-calendar-day probation period** starting on their hire date.
- Managers conduct structured check-ins at **Day 30**, **Day 60**, and **Day 90**.
- Successful completion of the Day 90 review is required to confirm regular employment status.

## 2. Day-1 Access and Mandatory Training
- Laptop provisioning, email credentials, and HR portal access are issued on **Day 1**.
- New joiners must complete Security Awareness and Workplace Safety training within their first **14 calendar days**.

## 3. Leave and Hybrid Work During Probation
- During the 90-day probation period, new joiners may take up to **2 paid leave days** total (drawn from their annual leave balance) without special escalation; any additional paid leave during probation requires joint written approval from the reporting manager and HR Partner.
- Routine weekly hybrid remote work (up to 2 remote days per week) becomes available **only after completing the 90-day probation period**, as detailed in `02_remote_and_hybrid_work_policy.pdf`.
- Note on benefits during probation: medical coverage begins on Day 1, while the USD 600 home-office setup reimbursement unlocks after 30 calendar days (see `03_benefits_and_reimbursement_policy.md`).
