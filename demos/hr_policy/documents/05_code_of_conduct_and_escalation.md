**Company:** Northstar Maritime Systems (Fictional) | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Workplace Conduct and Conflicts of Interest
- Employees must act with integrity, respect, and zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
- Actual or potential conflicts of interest (such as outside employment, vendor relationships, or family reporting lines) must be disclosed to HR within **10 business days** of awareness.

## 2. Escalation Ladder and Response SLAs
1. **Level 1 — Direct Manager:** Routine scheduling, leave requests, and day-to-day operational questions.
2. **Level 2 — HR Partner (`hr-support@northstar-synth.example`):** Policy interpretations, probation exceptions, and reimbursement disputes. Standard HR response SLA is **2 business days**.
3. **Level 3 — Confidential Ethics Channel (`ethics@northstar-synth.example`):** Harassment, safety violations, fraud, or retaliation reports. Initial acknowledgment is issued within **24 hours**, with zero tolerance for retaliation.

## 3. Scope Boundaries for Policy Guidance
- Policy guidance documents do not constitute legal advice or binding approval commitments.
- Individual salary adjustments, executive compensation figures, and disciplinary case files are confidential and excluded from general HR policy documentation.
