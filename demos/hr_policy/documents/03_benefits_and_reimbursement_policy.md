# Benefits and Reimbursement Policy (Synthetic Demonstration Data)

**Company:** Northstar Maritime Systems (Fictional) | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> Notice: This is synthetic demonstration data and not a real employment or benefits policy.

## 1. Medical and Health Coverage
- Full-time employees are enrolled in the company group medical, dental, and vision plan effective **Day 1 of employment**.
- Dependents may be added within **30 calendar days** of the start date or a qualifying life event.

## 2. Home-Office Reimbursement
- Employees who have completed **30 calendar days** of active service are eligible for a one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to **USD 600**.
- Eligible items include an ergonomic chair, external monitor, keyboard, or desk lamp. Internet bills and recurring utility charges are not covered under this one-time setup allowance.
- Itemized receipts must be submitted in the Expense Portal within **30 calendar days** of purchase.

## 3. Business Travel Meal Allowance
- During approved business travel, employees may claim meal reimbursements up to **USD 55 per travel day** with itemized receipts.
- Alcohol and personal entertainment expenses are non-reimbursable.
- Expense reports must be filed within **15 business days** after returning from travel.

## 4. Tuition and Certification Support
- Full-time employees with at least **12 months of continuous tenure** and a satisfactory performance rating may apply for up to **USD 2,500 per calendar year** in job-related tuition or professional certification reimbursement.
- Pre-approval from both the reporting manager and HR Partner is mandatory before course enrollment.
- Reimbursement is paid upon proof of passing completion.
