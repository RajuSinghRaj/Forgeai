Build a ship spares procurement assistant for operations and maintenance staff. It should answer questions about spare part availability, item specifications, supplier details, lead times, minimum order quantities and indicative costs using the supplied inventory CSV and supplier documents. It should retrieve exact catalogue records, combine information across documents where necessary, and calculate quantities and indicative totals when enough information is available. It must distinguish inventory stock from supplier availability, never claim that an order has been placed, and refuse to invent missing prices, specifications, stock figures or delivery commitments.
